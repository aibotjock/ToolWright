# Trademarks

## The software license does not grant trademark rights

This project is licensed under `Apache-2.0`. That license grants broad rights
over the **software**. It grants no rights over names, logos, or other brand
identifiers.

This is explicit in the license itself. Apache-2.0 section 6 states that the
license does not grant permission to use the trade names, trademarks, service
marks, or product names of the licensor.

**Under a permissive license, the trademark is the project's primary retained
asset.** Anyone may fork this code and build a commercial product on it. Nobody
may do so under this name.

## What you may do

Normal descriptive and nominative use is fine and requires no permission:

- Stating that your software is built with, based on, or compatible with MCP
  Inventor.
- Referring to Toolwright by name in documentation, articles, reviews,
  comparisons, tutorials, or academic work — favourable or critical.
- Linking to the project.
- Distributing unmodified copies under Apache-2.0 using the project's name.

## What requires permission

- Naming a **modified** version, fork, or derivative work "Toolwright", or
  using a name confusingly similar to it.
- Using the project name or logo as the name or branding of your product,
  service, company, or hosted offering.
- Using the name or logo in a way that suggests endorsement, affiliation, or
  official status that does not exist.
- Registering the name, a variation of it, or a corresponding domain in a way
  that could cause confusion about the origin of the software.

If you fork and modify the project, please give your fork its own name. You keep
every right Apache-2.0 gives you over the code; you simply need your own brand.

## Branding assets are separate from generated output

Branding assets are kept out of the code generator by design. Toolwright never
injects its name, logo, or marks into the projects it generates. Generated
projects are yours and carry your branding, not the platform's.

## Status

No claim is made here that any mark is registered. Rights in unregistered marks
arise from use and vary by jurisdiction.

The project name is a working name and may change. See
`packages/branding/src/index.ts`, which centralises every branded string so that
a rename requires editing one file.
